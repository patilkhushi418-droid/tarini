SAFETY GUIDE

If this is happening to you...

Here are some immediate things you can do. You do not need to open the chatbot to use these steps.

01
!

Someone is blackmailing me

Someone is threatening to share your photos, videos or personal information.

Do this first

  • Take screenshots of the threatening messages.
  • Save the text, username, phone number and profile URL.
  • If they have a photo or video, save the evidence and do not delete the chat.
02
◇

My photo is being misused

Your photo may have been edited, morphed, AI-generated or shared without your permission.

Do this first

  • Screenshot the post, profile or message.
  • Save the profile username and URL.
  • Keep the original photo if you have it and report the content.
03
@

Someone made a fake profile

Someone is using your name, photos or identity to create a fake account.

Do this first

  • Take screenshots of the fake profile.
  • Copy and save the profile URL and username.
  • Report the account for impersonation and keep the evidence.
04
!

I'm being harassed or threatened

You are receiving unwanted messages, threats, abuse or repeated contact.

Do this first

  • Save screenshots of messages and missed calls.
  • Keep usernames, phone numbers and dates as evidence.
  • Do not retaliate. If you feel unsafe, call 112.
